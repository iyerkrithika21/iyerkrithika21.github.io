I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the **Sheikh Zayed Institute for Pediatric Surgical Innovation at Children's National Hospital in Washington, D.C.** I work in the **Pediatric Accelerated Intelligence (PAI) Lab**, where I am advised by **Dr. Marius George Linguraru**.

I earned my Ph.D. from the **Scientific Computing and Imaging Institute at the University of Utah**, under the mentorship of **Dr. Shireen Elhabian**. I hold a Bachelor of Engineering from **Maharashtra Institute of Technology, University of Pune**, and have industry experience as an Associate System Engineer at **IBM Global Business Services**.

My research combines machine learning, probabilistic modeling, and statistical shape modeling to develop computational tools for medical image analysis. My current work focuses on pediatric imaging, including deep learning for ultra-low-field MRI and accessible imaging in low-resource settings. I also work on MRI-to-CT synthesis and brain tumor image analysis.

During my Ph.D., I developed unsupervised methods for anatomical shape modeling from surfaces and images and contributed to ShapeWorks, an open-source platform for statistical shape modeling.

I serve as an **organizer of the LISA Challenge at MICCAI (2025–2026)**. [Visit the LISA 2026 challenge](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn72118611/wiki/).
